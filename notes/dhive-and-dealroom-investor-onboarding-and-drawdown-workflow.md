## Overview

-   Investor onboarding and deal-room operations plan for DHive and dealroom.enlightencapital
-   Participants: Ashwin, Ranjan, Kausik, Manish, and Mandeep, with defined operational and advisory roles
-   Critical outcomes:
    -   Ashwin will serve as the primary investor point of contact and manage onboarding communications
    -   Ranjan will report to Ashwin and support investor coordination
    -   Initial deals will be manually prepared and uploaded for investor review
    -   Investor commitments will proceed from WhatsApp confirmation through portal KYC, commitment agreements, and drawdowns

## Investor Onboarding and Communication Ownership

-   Ashwin and Ranjan will be the first points of contact for investors onboarding through DHive and dealroom.enlightencapital
-   Ranjan will report directly to Ashwin
-   Ashwin will add registered dealroom investors and DHive investors to the WhatsApp group
-   Ashwin will post the welcome message and add Ranjan to the group
-   Ashwin will remain the single point of contact for investor communication through email, WhatsApp, and calls
-   Investor queries and welcome interactions will be handled manually to ensure investors receive human guidance rather than communicating only with the platform

## Deal-Room Setup and Initial Deal Pipeline

-   A prepaid SIM card has already been obtained as the single contact number for deal-room access
-   Deal projections will initially be handled manually
-   The first deals to be placed into the workflow are: Pontaq, BIAM, Biodimension, 3Miar, YatriKart
-   Ashwin will upload the executive summaries, decks, valuations, and due-diligence reports for investors

## Reporting, Advisory, and Escalation Structure

-   Ranjan will report to Ashwin within the deal-room operating structure
-   Ashwin will escalate matters to Kausik and Manish for advisory or opinion-based input
-   Mandeep will provide advisory support when Kausik or Manish are unavailable
-   Escalated investor or deal-related issues will therefore move from Ashwin to the designated advisory contacts

## Commitment, KYC, and Drawdown Process

-   Investors will initially communicate their intent to commit through WhatsApp by stating "commit"
-   Investors will then access the portal and complete the required data fields and KYC information
-   Completed portal information will lead to the commitment agreement and drawdown process
-   Ashwin will continue manual follow-up throughout the process
-   Investor support will cover consent collection, drawdown completion, and transfer of funds
