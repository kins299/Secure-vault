Enlighten Capital - Board Call-2 (November 05, 2026)



November 05, 2026
Attendees: amrish@enlightencapital.in, ashwin@enlightencapital.in, contactrajkapoor@gmail.com, goutham.s@enlightencapital.in, koushik.s@enlightencapital.in, mandeep@enlightencapital.in, Mandeep Singh, maneesh.s@enlightencapital.in, maneesh@alphavalue.co.in

Overview
Enlighten Capital reviewed recent investments, fundraising, and plans for healthcare investing and a syndication platform.
Fund Activity
- About ₹3.25 crore has been deployed across four startups; roughly ₹1 crore more is in the pipeline for the coming weeks, with a fifth investment in progress.
- LP tickets range from ₹2.5 lakh to ₹15 lakh for individual angels, with larger UHNI commitments reaching ₹1 crore. Mandeep requested recurring updates on total corpus, commitments, and month-over-month LP growth.
- Mandeep emphasized co-investing alongside established lead investors for early-stage deals to reduce risk. Early-stage candidates are expected to complete evaluation in about 10 days; deal-room onboarding was suggested for joint review.
- Chennai-based investors are interested in short-term opportunities, including IPO or secondary deals with a potential six-to-12-month exit, and validated early-stage companies using capital for growth. A working-capital debt opportunity was also raised: a supplier with a ₹1,500 crore state-government order seeks about ₹300 crore; potential lender fit and collateral require review.
Programs
- Bharat Health Investments for Innovation (BHI 2) is being developed as a healthcare and deep-tech co-investment program. MOUs are in place or under discussion with Bangalore Bio Innovation Center and STPI; discussions also include Unicorn India Ventures, Pan IIT Andhra Fund, Blue Hill, and IIT Madras. The proposed Enlighten allocation is about ₹25 crore, contributing to a broader ₹100–150 crore pool for 10–15 startups with TRL 3+ and valid IP.
- One Pitch is proposed as a syndication platform connecting 100–200 VC partners and family offices. IVCA is evaluating the concept; potential revenue includes shared management and success fees. Mandeep noted that deal-sharing norms and fee expectations may be adoption barriers.
- Mandeep reported that the Indian Venture Studio Association (IVSA) is being formed and said Enlighten could use IVCA and IVSA networks for co-investment and venture-studio deal flow.
Deal Room
- Koushik described a selective LP network and limited responses to posted deals. Mandeep recommended a regular cadence for presenting a vetted startup, hosting a founder pitch and Q&A, collecting investment interest, and sharing commitment totals with other LPs.
- Open question: What recurring cadence and engagement format will generate sufficient LP participation to meet the ₹25 lakh minimum investment threshold?
Action Items
- Share the meeting recording with Koushik and the full team after the call.
- Establish a recurring LP update covering total committed corpus, amount raised to date, and additions during the month or quarter, beginning with the next update.
- Form a group of relevant family-office and UHNI investors for sharing suitable pre-IPO and secondary deals. (Mandeep Singh)
- Complete evaluation of the current early-stage startup list within 10 days and share the evaluated startups as a link for joint review.
- Share the working-capital debt opportunity and related details with Koushik offline. (Mandeep Singh)
- Prepare and share a short one-pager or deck for the medical-equipment working-capital debt opportunity with the relevant investor group, subject to reviewing the deal details.
- Email the One Pitch materials as a consolidated one-page link with the relevant documents included.
- Review the IVSA links and identify potential collaboration opportunities for building Enlighten's startup pipeline.
- Organize a deal-room call after Diwali to engage current and prospective LPs and demonstrate the deal-room portal.
Outline
Fund Performance and LP Reporting
- Reported approximately ₹3.25 crore deployed across four startups, with about ₹1 crore in the pipeline and a fifth investment underway.
- Discussed LP ticket sizes, investor preferences, and the request for recurring corpus and fundraising updates.
Startup Deal Flow
- Discussed early-stage evaluations, the value of lead-investor participation, and sharing deals through the deal room.
- Covered short-term IPO and secondary opportunities, validated growth-stage companies, and a working-capital debt opportunity tied to a ₹1,500 crore government order.
BHI 2 Healthcare Program
- Reviewed the proposed co-investment strategy, target corpus of ₹100–150 crore, and target of 10–15 deep-tech startups with TRL 3+ and valid IP.
- Discussed institutional relationships and pipeline development with BBC, STPI, Unicorn India Ventures, Pan IIT Andhra Fund, Blue Hill, and IIT Madras.
One Pitch and Industry Networks
- Discussed a proposed syndication platform for 100–200 VC partners and family offices, with potential fee-sharing revenue.
- Raised cultural and commercial questions about deal sharing and success-fee arrangements; Mandeep shared IVSA network context.
LP Deal-Room Engagement
- Discussed low response rates and the need for a consistent, curated deal-presentation process with founder Q&A and clear commitment updates.
- The group had not yet settled on a cadence or format.

### Fundraising Strategy & LP Onboarding

- [**Model:** Deal-by-deal angel fund, similar to Let's Venture.](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=315.0 "PLAY @5:15")
- [**LP Commitment:**](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=236.0 "PLAY @3:56")
    - [**Current:** ₹25 lakh minimum commitment over 5 years.](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=236.0 "PLAY @3:56")
    - [**Mechanism:** No upfront cash; LPs invest on a deal-by-deal basis.](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=300.0 "PLAY @5:00")
- [**Fee Structure:**](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=375.0 "PLAY @6:15")
    - [**Current:** 2% management fee + 20% carry (2/20).](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=375.0 "PLAY @6:15")
    - [**Proposed:** 0% management fee + 20% carry (0/20).](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=375.0 "PLAY @6:15")
        - [**Rationale:** A 0% management fee is more attractive to LPs and aligns with the deal-by-deal model, which lacks a blind pool for a traditional fee.](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=441.0 "PLAY @7:21")
        - [**Trigger:** Carry is calculated per-deal, triggered by a 1.5x return hurdle.](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=375.0 "PLAY @6:15")
    - [**Action:** The Private Placement Memorandum (PPM) will be revised by year-end to reflect this new structure.](https://fathom.video/share/tefg48aVqy5nViBmvzvdVQcsx1d-PedL?tab=summary&timestamp=375.0 "PLAY @6:15")
