# **Enlighten Capital — Board Meeting Detailed Summary**
**Date:** November 05, 2026  
**Attendees:** Amrish, Ashwin, Raj Kapoor, Goutham S., Koushik S., Mandeep Singh, Maneesh S.  
**Primary Purpose:** Align on LP engagement models, restructure fundraising fees and investor onboarding, evaluate strategic fund vehicles (BHI 2), and finalize plans for upcoming ecosystem events (TiE Global Summit, Bangalore Tech Summit).

---

## 1. Comprehensive Discussion Points

### A. Fund Performance, Deployments & Pipeline
* **Current Deployments:** Enlighten Capital has deployed approximately **₹3.25 crore across four startups**, with ~₹1 crore currently in the pipeline and closing underway for a fifth startup investment.
* **Investor Allocation Bands:**
  * **Angel Investors:** Allocations range from ₹2.5 lakh to ₹15 lakh.
  * **UHNIs / High-Capacity Investors:** Deploying up to ₹1 crore per deal.
* **Special Debt Opportunity:** 
  * The board reviewed an opportunistic debt proposal: a **₹300 crore working-capital financing request** backing an existing ₹1,500 crore government procurement order for medical and critical equipment supplies.
  * *Status:* Mandeep is vetting the deal parameters offline and will present a structured one-pager for selective UHNI/family office participation.

---

### B. Fundraising Strategy, Investor Model & PPM Restructuring
* **Structural Model:** Enlighten operates as a **deal-by-deal angel fund** (similar to the syndication mechanics of platforms like Let's Venture):
  * **LP Commitment:** Minimum ₹25 lakh commitment spread across a 5-year period.
  * **Drawdowns:** No upfront capital lock-in; funds are called only when an LP opts into an individual deal.
* **Fee Structure Pivot (Transitioning from 2/20 to 0/20):**
  * **The Challenge:** The traditional 2% management fee on committed capital was causing friction and slowdowns during LP onboarding.
  * **The Resolution:** Enlighten is transitioning to a **0% management fee + 20% carried interest (0/20)** deal-by-deal structure.
  * **Hurdle Rate:** Carried interest will only be triggered after delivering a **1.5x capital return** to investors on that specific deal.
  * **Documentation:** The Private Placement Memorandum (PPM) and legal agreements are being amended to formally reflect this new 0/20 carry-only model by the end of the year.

---

### C. LP Engagement & Deal-Room Activation
* **Issue Identified:** While LPs expressed interest, attendance and engagement inside the digital deal-room platform remained suboptimal due to inconsistent scheduling.
* **The "Enlighten's Time" Initiative:**
  * To build brand habit and top-of-mind recall, Enlighten will institute a permanent, recurring weekly or bi-weekly pitch call slot (targeting Saturday mornings, e.g., 11:00 AM).
  * A poll will be floated immediately to prospective and current LPs offering 2–3 Saturday windows to lock in the community's preferred recurring slot.
* **Post-Diwali Re-Engagement:** A dedicated platform walkthrough and founder pitch session will be hosted post-Diwali to re-activate deal-room signups.

---

### D. Strategic Programs & Syndication Initiatives
* **Bharat Health Investments for Innovation (BHI 2):**
  * **Target Size:** ₹100–150 crore healthcare and deep-tech co-investment vehicle, with **₹25 crore committed from Enlighten Capital**.
  * **Portfolio Target:** 10–15 early-to-growth startups with proven validation (Technology Readiness Level / TRL 3+ and defensible, registered IP).
  * **Soft Launch:** Scheduled for the **Bangalore Tech Summit (BTS)** in collaboration with the Bangalore Bioinnovation Centre (BBC) via a shared exhibition booth.
* **One Pitch Platform & IVSA:**
  * Enlighten is exploring a strategic alliance with IVCA (Indian Venture and Alternate Capital Association) for the **One Pitch** syndication engine (providing distribution to 100–200 VCs and family offices).
  * Synergies are also being built with the newly forming **Indian Venture Studio Association (IVSA)** network to source co-build and venture studio deals.

---

### E. Events & Ecosystem Alignments
* **TiE Global Summit (Indore — Dec 13–15, 2026):**
  * Koushik secured **4 complimentary delegate passes** for the Enlighten core team.
  * Mandeep will be attending in an official capacity as a pitch jury member.
  * **In-Person Board Meeting:** The board agreed to leverage the summit to conduct Enlighten’s next formal board meeting face-to-face in Indore.
  * **Partnership Discussion:** A strategic partnership with Abhishek (Ecosystem Venture) will be explored ahead of and during the summit.
