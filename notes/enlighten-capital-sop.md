## 2. Fund Governance & Organizational Roles

```
[ Sponsors / Trustees ]
           │
           ▼
[ Investment Manager (IM) ] ◄──► [ Advisory Board / Legal & Compliance ]
           │
           ├───────────────┬───────────────┐
           ▼               ▼               ▼
   [ Sourcing & DD ]    [ IC ]   [ Operations & Investor Relations ]
```

* **Trustee:** Oversees regulatory compliance, safekeeping of assets, and fiduciary responsibilities.
* **Investment Manager (IM):** Manages day-to-day sourcing, underwriting, transaction execution, and portfolio monitoring.
* **Investment Committee (IC):** Evaluates screened deals, issues preliminary approvals, and authorises term sheets.
* **Angel Investors (LPs):** Accreditated investors who retain deal-by-deal opt-in/approval rights as per Angel Fund regulations.

## 3. Investor (LP) Onboarding & KYC Procedure

### 3.1 Eligibility Check (Regulatory Criteria)
Before onboarding an investor as an Angel Investor into Enlighten Capital:
- [ ] **Individual Angel Investor:** Minimum net tangible assets of ₹2 Cr (excluding principal residence), with documented early-stage investment experience or management experience.
- [ ] **Corporate Angel Investor:** Net worth of at least ₹10 Cr.
- [ ] **AIF / Qualified Institutional Buyer (QIB):** Appropriate fund documents verifying registration status.

### 3.2 Onboarding Workflow

| Stage | Action Items | Responsible Party | TAT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Step 1: Initiation** | Provide Information Memorandum (PIM/PPM) and investor application form. | Investor Relations (IR) | Day 1 |
| **Step 2: KYC & AML** | Collect PAN, Aadhaar/Passport, CKYC number, FATCA/CRS self-certification, bank details. | Compliance Team | Day 2–3 |
| **Step 3: Verification** | Run AML/PEP checks, verify accredited investor status, confirm bank account via penny drop. | Ops & Compliance | Day 4–5 |
| **Step 4: Execution** | Execute Contribution Agreement and Power of Attorney (PoA). Issue Welcome Kit and Portal access. | IR / Legal | Day 6–7 |

## 4. Deal Sourcing & Pipeline Management

```
Sourcing ──► Initial Screening ──► Partner Meeting ──► IC Review ──► Term Sheet
```

1. **Inbound & Outbound Sourcing:** Deals logged in CRM from founders, venture partners, syndicates, and demo days.
2. **Preliminary Screening Checklist:**
   - **Ticket Size:** Fits Angel Fund constraints (e.g., minimum ₹25 Lakhs up to regulatory maximums per deal).
   - **Stage:** Pre-Seed, Seed, Pre-Series A.
   - **Target Company Criteria:** Meets startup eligibility criteria (e.g., incorporation within 10 years, turnover limits).
3. **Internal Partner Review:** Preliminary financial model, cap table, and founder background check. If viable, sponsor the deal to the **Investment Committee (IC)**.

## 5. Due Diligence (DD) Framework

Once the IC clears the preliminary pitch, formal diligence begins:

| DD Stream | Key Areas of Verification | External/Internal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Commercial DD** | Market sizing, customer calls, product-market fit, unit economics. | Internal Investment Team |
| **Financial & Tax DD** | Historical accounts, bank reconciliation, tax liabilities, runway, valuation sanity. | External CA / Accounting Firm |
| **Legal & Regulatory DD** | Corporate records, IP ownership, ESOP pool, prior shareholder agreements, litigations. | External Legal Counsel |
| **Founder Diligence** | Background check, reference checks from previous colleagues and investors. | Ops / Independent Agency |

## 6. IC Approval & Angel Investor (LP) Syndication

Because Category I Angel Funds require **deal-by-deal investor opt-in**:

```
[ IC Approval Memo ] 
        │
        ▼
[ Deal Summary Note circulated to LPs ]
        │
        ├──────────────────────────────┐
        ▼                              ▼
  [ LP Opts In ]                [ LP Declines / No Action ]
  Commitment Form Signed                 Excluded from Deal
        │
        ▼
[ Aggregate Commitments Finalized ]
```

1. **IC Approval:** IC signs off on the final valuation, proposed ticket size, and SHA/SSA rights.
2. **Deal Memorandum to LPs:** 
   - A standardized Deal Memorandum (Company Overview, IC Thesis, Valuation, DD Summary, Risks) is shared with LPs.
   - A binding commitment deadline is defined (typically 5 to 7 business days).
3. **Commitment Threshold:** Minimum capital aggregation must meet the fund’s minimum deal threshold (e.g., minimum ₹25 Lakhs total syndicate size per regulatory norms).

## 7. Execution, Capital Drawdown & Settlement

### 7.1 Capital Calls (Drawdown)
1. **Notice Issuance:** Ops team issues a formal Drawdown Notice to opted-in investors with:
   - Committed amount
   - Deal-specific management fee / setup fee contribution
   - Bank escrow account details & strict transfer deadline (usually 3–5 working days).
2. **Receipt Reconciliation:** Escrow bank confirms receipt of funds; Ops matches funds against committed LPs.

### 7.2 Transaction Closing
1. **Definitive Agreements:** Finalization and execution of **Share Subscription Agreement (SSA)** and **Shareholders’ Agreement (SHA)**.
2. **Fund Remittance:** Trustee / Custodian authorizes transfer of aggregate subscription funds from Fund Escrow to the portfolio startup’s escrow/current account.
3. **Allotment & Filings:**
   - Portfolio company files **PAS-3** (Return of Allotment) with RoC.
   - Startup issues share certificates or credits demat shares to the Fund Custody Account within the statutory period (max 60 days).

## 8. Portfolio Monitoring & Post-Investment Governance

* **Information Rights & MIS Tracking:**
  - **Monthly/Quarterly MIS:** Founders must submit financial metrics (Revenue, Burn, Runway, Headcount, MoM Growth) by the 15th of the following month.
  - **Board Representation / Observership:** Nominated partner attends board meetings and files quarterly updates for the IC.
* **Investor Reporting:**
  - Consolidated quarterly newsletter to LPs highlighting portfolio performance.
  - Annual audited financials and performance metrics (IRR, MOIC, NAV).
* **Valuation of Portfolio:** Annual fair-market valuation through an independent SEBI-registered valuer / Merchant Banker.

---

## 9. Exit Strategy & Liquidation Workflow

```
Exit Trigger ──► IC Recommendation ──► Execution ──► Proceeds to Escrow ──► LP Distribution
```

1. **Triggers:** Secondary sale during next funding rounds, strategic acquisition/M&A, Founder Buyback, or IPO.
2. **Process:**
   - IM negotiates exit terms/purchase agreement.
   - IC reviews and formally approves the exit price.
   - Trustee approves the share transfer.
3. **Distribution Waterfall:**
   - Gross proceeds deposited into the Fund Distribution Escrow.
   - Deductions: Deal-related legal/advisory expenses, fund management carry/performance fees (if applicable), and withholding taxes.
   - Net proceeds distributed pro-rata to the specific LPs who opted into that company within **T+5 business days** of realization.
