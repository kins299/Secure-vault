## Monthly Evaluation Cycle (1st to 10th)

-   Ashwin and Kaushik review registered startups in the portal/deal room
-   Startups matching the thesis are shortlisted and brought to the evaluation committee

## Committee Review (11th to 30th)

-   Evaluation committee assesses shortlisted startups from about the 15th/16th through the 30th
-   Delivers a report by the 30th (or 1st of the following month) naming up to 3 startups to present to investors

## Investor Presentation and Response (First Weekend + Week 2)

-   Top 3 startups presented on the first weekend with a tech investor memo and a link to full data, projections and current statistics
-   Investors given one week to respond
-   Second-week Saturday: poll sent asking for yes/no interest per deal
    -   If yes: asked whether a deep dive call is needed
    -   If deep dive requested: founders call scheduled for further analysis

## Post-Interest Process and 60-Day Target

-   After the deep dive, investors begin committing funds
-   Workstreams initiated: commitment agreements, drawdown notices, capital call/money projection
-   Target: end-to-end completion within 60 days of startup registration
-   If no: feedback collected on reasons (thesis mismatch, red flags, etc.) and a report shared with the startup for learning and process improvement
