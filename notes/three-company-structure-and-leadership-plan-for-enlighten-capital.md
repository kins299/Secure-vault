## Enlighten Angel Fund: Near-Complete Fund Structure

-   Current readiness: formulation, compliances, people, and organizational structure are approximately 75% complete
-   Additional directors are required to strengthen the fund's ability to raise capital
-   At least one incoming director should have strong experience in fund management and fund structuring
-   These appointments are expected to bring the fund to approximately 90–95% completion
-   The fund is being kept structurally separate from the other companies

## Citrus Source One Technology: Layered Leadership and Operating Model

-   Gautam currently heads Citrus Source One Technology
-   Ranjan Saktivel participates with a 5% stake in the company
-   The company should be organized across approximately five or six structural layers
-   A core leadership team is needed with expertise in BPDP, CI, fund compliances, and technology-related activities
-   A flow diagram should define the first, second, third, and fourth layers, along with the leadership roles above and below them
-   Gautam should evaluate each competent person during discussions and determine where that person fits within the proposed structure
-   Leadership recruitment should begin after the structure identifies the required roles and reporting levels

## IDAX Grow-up: CEO Recruitment and CSR Funding Model

-   IDAX Grow-up is intended to operate as an NGO company
-   A 30-day period is planned to identify one highly competent chief executive officer
-   The CEO should understand how to obtain CSR funds, which partnerships to establish, how funding will enter the organization, and how to create a sustainable funding cycle
-   The operating model must define how Enlighten Capital can contribute to IDAX Grow-up, how IDAX Grow-up can contribute to Citrus Source One Technology, and how Citrus Source One Technology can support both sides
-   The objective is an integrated, mutually reinforcing structure across the three companies

## Consolidated Governance Across All Three Companies

-   Leadership teams from Enlighten Angel Fund, Citrus Source One Technology, and IDAX Grow-up should review the combined structure every two or three months
-   Reviews should focus on improving coordination, funding flows, leadership effectiveness, and the overall use of the three companies
-   The combined structure is expected to determine how effectively all three entities can be utilized
